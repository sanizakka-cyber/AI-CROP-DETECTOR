# Reviewer guide: checking MSAS FarmAI results

## English

**What this is.** MSAS FarmAI gives farmers advice from a photo of a crop, animal, soil or pest. We translate the result into your language and read it aloud. Before farmers see it, we need you to check that the words are right and the voice is clear. One pack has 25 results. Each takes about 10 minutes.

**1. Sign in**
1. Open the invitation email from MSAS FarmAI and press **Set my password**. The link works for 7 days.
2. Choose a password only you know. MSAS staff will never ask you for it.
3. Open the review page linked in the email. You will see your review packs.

**2. For each result**
1. Read the English text on the left and your language on the right.
2. Press play on **Voice A**, **Voice B** and **Voice C**. Listen to each one fully. We do not tell you which voice is which, on purpose.
3. Give the scores (1 is very bad, 5 is very good):

   | Score | Accuracy (the words) | Naturalness (the voice) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 5 | Same meaning, nothing missing, sounds like a person from here wrote it | Sounds like a real person talking |
   | 4 | Same meaning, small wording problems | Clear, a little machine-like |
   | 3 | Mostly right, one part unclear | Understandable but tiring |
   | 2 | Meaning changed in one place | Hard to follow |
   | 1 | Wrong or dangerous | Cannot understand |

   Also score **pronunciation** and **speed** for each voice.
4. Choose the voice you would want a farmer to hear.

**3. Listen especially for**
- **Numbers, doses and units:** "2.5 g per litre", "every 7 days". A wrong number is the most serious error.
- **Medicine, chemical and product names:** they must not be changed or mispronounced.
- **The safety note:** it must still say to confirm with an extension officer or vet.
- **The names MSAS FarmAI, GPS and LGA:** they are in the **Pronunciations** item.
- The **Numbers and units**, **Screen labels**, **Spoken safety note** and **Pronunciations** items at the end of the pack.

**4. Flag an error**
1. Select the wrong words with your finger or mouse, then press **Mark selected text as an error**.
2. Choose the type: **Wrong dose, amount or unit**, **Wrong chemical, medicine or product**, Meaning changed or unclear, Grammar or spelling, Mispronounced in the audio, or Other.
3. Write the correct words in the note. When the item is done, press **Save and go to the next item**.

Any dose or chemical error stops the language going live until it is fixed. Thank you: your ear protects farmers.

## Hausa

**Menene wannan.** MSAS FarmAI na ba manoma shawara daga hoton amfanin gona, dabba, ƙasa ko kwari. Muna fassara sakamakon zuwa harshenku kuma muna karanta shi da murya. Kafin manoma su gani, muna son ka duba cewa kalmomin daidai ne kuma muryar a fili take. Kunshi ɗaya yana da sakamako 25. Kowanne yana ɗaukar kusan minti 10.

**1. Shiga**
1. Buɗe saƙon gayyata daga MSAS FarmAI, ka danna **Set my password**. Mahaɗin yana aiki kwana 7.
2. Zaɓi kalmar sirri da kai kaɗai ka sani. Ma'aikatan MSAS ba za su taɓa tambayarka ita ba.
3. Buɗe shafin dubawa da ke cikin saƙon. Za ka ga kunshin da za ka duba.

**2. Ga kowane sakamako**
1. Karanta rubutun Turanci a hagu da na harshenka a dama.
2. Danna kunna **Murya A**, **Murya B** da **Murya C**. Saurari kowace har ƙarshe. Ba mu faɗi wace murya ce ta wa ba, da gangan.
3. Ba da maki (1 mummuna ƙwarai, 5 kyakkyawa ƙwarai):

   | Maki | Daidaito (kalmomi) | Sauƙin ji (murya) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 5 | Ma'ana ɗaya, babu abin da ya ɓace, kamar mutumin nan ne ya rubuta | Kamar mutum na gaske yana magana |
   | 4 | Ma'ana ɗaya, ƙananan matsalolin kalmomi | A fili, kaɗan kamar na'ura |
   | 3 | Galibi daidai, wani sashi bai fito fili ba | Ana fahimta amma yana gajiyarwa |
   | 2 | Ma'ana ta canza a wuri ɗaya | Da wuya a bi |
   | 1 | Ba daidai ba ko mai haɗari | Ba a fahimta |

   Haka kuma ba da maki ga **furuci** da **sauri** na kowace murya.
4. Zaɓi muryar da za ka so manomi ya ji.

**3. Ka saurara musamman**
- **Lambobi, yawan magani da ma'auni:** "2.5 g a kowace lita", "kowace kwana 7". Lamba da ba daidai ba ita ce kuskure mafi muni.
- **Sunayen magani, sinadarai da kayayyaki:** kada a canza su ko a faɗe su ba daidai ba.
- **Sanarwar tsaro:** dole ta faɗi a tabbatar da jami'in faɗakarwa ko likitan dabbobi.
- **Sunayen MSAS FarmAI, GPS da LGA:** suna cikin abin **Pronunciations**.
- Abubuwan **Numbers and units** (lambobi), **Screen labels** (rubutun allo), **Spoken safety note** (sanarwar tsaro) da **Pronunciations** (furuci) a ƙarshen kunshin.

**4. Nuna kuskure**
1. Zaɓi kalmomin da ba daidai ba da yatsa ko linzamin kwamfuta, sannan danna **Mark selected text as an error**.
2. Zaɓi irin kuskuren: **Wrong dose, amount or unit** (yawan magani), **Wrong chemical, medicine or product** (sinadari ko magani), ma'ana, nahawu, furuci, ko wani.
3. Rubuta kalmomin da suka dace a cikin bayanin. Idan ka gama, danna **Save and go to the next item**.

Duk wani kuskuren yawan magani ko sinadari yana hana harshen fara aiki har sai an gyara. Mun gode: kunnenka yana kare manoma.

*(The Hausa text needs a check by a native speaker before it is sent to reviewers.)*
